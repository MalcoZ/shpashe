Driftfield

A living field of light with Hubble nebulae drifting through it. Stir it, tune it, and play it.

Play

  • Move or drag to stir the current and send ripples across it.
  • What your finger does changes with each preset. It may stir, swirl, send ripples, pull light toward it, or paint color, and with sound on it may sweep the filter, bend the pitch, change the rhythm, widen the echo, or chop the gate. The Touch setting picks the mode.
  • Scroll or pinch to change the terrain.
  • Press and hold to freeze a moment.
  • Tilt your phone to steer the flow.
  • Drop an image onto the page to add it to the visions.

The dock

The arrows step through presets, each a full look and sound; a dot beside the number means you've changed it since. Shuffle picks a new look. Chaos lets every setting drift on its own. The speaker turns the drone on or off. The camera records a short clip and saves it as a GIF that plays forward and back. The sliders icon opens every setting.
